Aftercare

      
Heal it right.

      
A great tattoo heals well when it is looked after. A simple guide, plus reminders in your phone calendar.

    

    


First hours
Keep it covered
Leave the wrap on for as long as the studio tells you. Wash your hands before touching the area.


Days 1–3
Wash gently
Lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap, pat dry with a clean paper towel, then a very thin layer of the product your artist recommends.


Days 4–14
Itch, do not scratch
Light peeling and itching are normal. Do not pick or scratch. Moisturise lightly with a fragrance-free lotion.


Weeks 2–4
Keep protecting
The surface looks healed while the skin keeps settling underneath. Stay out of direct sun, and use SPF 50 once it is healed.


Avoid
For about two weeks
No pools, sea, baths or sauna, no direct sun, and no tight or rough clothes rubbing on the tattoo, unless your artist says otherwise.


Worried?
When to get help
Spreading redness, heat, swelling, pus, fever or pain that grows after the first days: contact the studio and a doctor.

Questions, answered.

    

      
Good to know

      

      
How much does a tattoo cost?
It depends on size, detail and time. Send your idea with spot and size and we will give you a quote.

      
Can I bring my own reference?
Yes. Photos of the person, pet or style you like help a lot. We adapt the design to your body.

      
Can you draw something custom for me?
Yes. Send your idea and any references and the studio will draw a design for you, adapted to your body.

      
Can I choose a ready-made design?
Yes. Ask on Instagram which designs are available, or send your own idea.

      
Can I just walk in?
Opening hours change, so message us on Instagram first to check availability. For bigger pieces we recommend booking.

      
Do I need to pay a deposit?
When we agree the quote we will tell you whether a deposit applies and which payment methods we accept.

      
How many sessions will I need?
Small pieces usually take one session. Large or very detailed work, like sleeves or back pieces, can take several. We will estimate it in your quote.

      
Does it hurt?
It depends on the spot and on you. Ribs, hands, feet and neck are usually more sensitive than the upper arm or thigh. Ask us and we will tell you what to expect.

      
How should I prepare?
Sleep well, eat before you come, drink water and avoid alcohol the day before. Wear clothes that give easy access to the area.

      
How do I look after it afterwards?
We give you clear care instructions after the session. In general: keep it clean, do not pick or scratch it, and avoid sun, pools and the sea while it heals.

      
Can I change the design after booking?
Yes, tell us as early as possible and we will adjust it before the session.

      
Are there age rules?
Yes. If you are under 18, message us before booking so we can explain what is needed.
